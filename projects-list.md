# Project and Product Inventory

This file is an editorial working list for the portfolio and professional profile. It is not a claim that every item is a released product or a public repository.

Use these rules when selecting work for public presentation:

- Describe the work actually completed, not only the intended product.
- Label projects as shipped, active development, prototype, research or archived.
- Do not imply customer adoption, revenue, scale or measurable impact without evidence.
- Do not publish private customer information, internal pricing, credentials, private RFP material or proprietary implementation details.
- Link only to repositories and demos that are public and approved for disclosure.

## Evidence needed for stronger case studies

For each selected project, collect:

1. Business problem and intended users.
2. Your actual role and individual contribution.
3. What was implemented versus proposed.
4. Architecture and important technical trade-offs.
5. Integrations, deployment and operational responsibilities.
6. Public repository/demo or approved screenshots.
7. Verified outcomes, if measured.
8. What remains incomplete or unverified.

## Portfolio editorial rule

Write in first person and use concrete verbs: built, integrated, migrated, tested, deployed, maintained, reviewed, designed. Avoid generic claims such as “transformed businesses”, “revolutionized workflows”, “cutting-edge solutions” or “seamlessly bridged innovation and execution” unless a specific, evidenced result makes the wording meaningful.
